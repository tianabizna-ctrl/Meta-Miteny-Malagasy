Pronunciation guide

Malagasy is phonetic: words sound the way they look. Each vowel keeps one clear sound.

a

ah

like father

e

eh

like bet

i

ee

like see

o

oo

like go

u

oo

like food

  • 1Stress: usually on the second-to-last syllable — ma-NAO, mi-SOW-trah.
  • 2Trilled r: let the tip of your tongue tap lightly, like a soft rolling r in reraka.
  • 3Final y: sounds like ee — tsara ends with a soft rah, not why.
  • 4Try it: say Salama slowly — sah-LAH-mah — then smile as you say it.

Cultural tips

  • • Use Tompoko — a respectful “Sir / Madam” — with elders and people you have just met.
  • • Greet first. A warm handshake and Salama open almost every conversation.
  • • Respect fady — local taboos and customs. When unsure, ask kindly: “Azafady, mety ve?”
  • • Avoid pointing with one finger. Use an open hand or a gentle nod instead.
  • • Take your time. A little Malagasy, said with a smile, goes a very long way.
